Frequently asked

A task was due and nothing happened.

Four things, in order of how often they are the cause:

  • Notification permission. Settings › Doorbell › Notifications — Allow Notifications on, and Lock Screen, Notification Centre and Banners all ticked.
  • Focus or Do Not Disturb. Doorbell marks its due alerts as time-sensitive so they can break through a Focus, but a Focus can still be set to block them. In Settings › Focus › (your focus) › Apps, allow Doorbell, and leave “Time Sensitive Notifications” on.
  • Silent switch or volume. The doorbell is a sound; the ring/silent switch and the ringer volume both apply.
  • The task had no due time. Only tasks with a time on the Day view ring. A task with a date but no time just sits in the list.

The assistant says Apple Intelligence is not available.

The on-device assistant uses Apple’s Foundation Models, which need iOS 26 and an Apple Intelligence-capable device, with Apple Intelligence switched on in Settings › Apple Intelligence & Siri. On a device that cannot run it, Doorbell quietly falls back to its own rules-based parser — that is not an error, and phrases like “gym tomorrow at 7” or “call mum every day at 18:00” still work. The more conversational requests are the ones you will miss.

How do I connect my own LLM (Ollama)?

Run the server on a machine on the same network, then point Doorbell at it:

  • On the machine running Ollama, make it listen beyond localhost: OLLAMA_HOST=0.0.0.0:11434 ollama serve, and pull a model, e.g. ollama pull llama3.2.
  • Find that machine’s address on your network (something like 192.168.1.20).
  • In Doorbell: Settings › Assistant › Local server. Enter http://192.168.1.20:11434 and the model name, then tap Test.
  • The first time, iOS asks for permission to find devices on your local network. Say yes, or the connection will silently fail.

Anything with an OpenAI-compatible /v1/chat/completions endpoint works the same way. This is the only network connection Doorbell ever makes, and only because you set it up.

How do I push a task back?

Swipe or long-press the notification and pick +15 min, or open the task and use the extend buttons: +5, +15, +30, +60. You can extend before it is due as well as after — the countdown simply resets and the doorbell will ring again at the new time.

The widget or Live Activity is not showing.

The Live Activity appears on the lock screen and in the Dynamic Island only when you have a task due later today; it disappears once the day’s tasks are done. Check Settings › Doorbell › Live Activities is on.

The widget is added the usual way: long-press the home screen, tap the +, search for Doorbell, and drag the size you want. It refreshes when tasks change and on the system’s own schedule, so give it a minute after a change.

Do my tasks sync between my iPhone and my Mac?

No. There is no sync and no cloud, by design — there is no server to sync through. Each device keeps its own list.

How do I delete everything?

Delete the app. Everything Doorbell knows lives inside the app on your device, so removing it removes the lot. There is no account to close and no copy held anywhere else.
