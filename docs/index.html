Doorbell

A to-do list that rings when the task is actually due.

PrivacySupport

Most to-do apps show you a list and hope you look at it. Doorbell waits for the minute a task is due and then rings a doorbell — on the lock screen, in the Dynamic Island, and as a notification you can answer without opening the app.

Mark it done, push it back by fifteen minutes, dismiss it or delete it, straight from the alert.

What it does

  • Day, Week, Month and Recurring views, ranked by priority then by due time
  • A live countdown on everything due today
  • Extend by +5, +15, +30 or +60 minutes when life gets in the way
  • A daily “plan your day” prompt
  • A home-screen widget and a Live Activity for whatever is next
  • An assistant you can talk to in plain English: “gym tomorrow at 7”, “call mum every day at 18:00”, “done with laundry”

Where the thinking happens

On iPhone and iPad the assistant runs on Apple Intelligence — Apple’s on-device Foundation Models — so what you type never leaves the device. That needs iOS 26 and an Apple Intelligence-capable device. Without it, a built-in rules parser understands the same phrasings, offline. There is also a Mac version, built from the same code.

Privacy, in one line

No account, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no server to send anything to. Your tasks live in a database on your device. The full policy is short.
